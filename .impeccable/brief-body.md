# Portfolio home (single page)

Scope: the whole one-page portfolio (`src/App.tsx` and its sections). Visitor mode: Experience, with a Persuade close (contact).

Audience: recruiters, engineering leads, freelance and contract clients, arriving cold. Action: email or LinkedIn. Proof: resume metrics stitched to role and date, the scripted clinical-pipeline replay, shipped systems. Constraints: assistant is a scripted keyword guide and says so; no invented metrics; project links go to the GitHub profile.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a night market at dusk. A river of lit bulbs leads the visitor past hand-painted stalls, one per shipped system, to the counter where they can talk to Sworup. It refuses the neon-on-black developer portfolio with glass cards and gradient text.

OWN-WORLD: Wet-asphalt black ground with grain; saffron bulb light as the only glow; flat sign inks (awning blue, banner red, marigold, steam white); rough brush-edged sign boards and marker price cards; scalloped striped awnings. Brush display lettering, marker labels, a plain grotesque for body, mono only for real data.

STORY: Within one viewport the visitor knows who he is (AI/ML + fullstack, clinical software). Walking the river they see stalls (projects), watch the kitchen replay turn dictation into a record, read the menu board (skills), the workshop ledger (roles, dated metrics), then reach the counter and email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A sagging string of bulbs spans the top and lights one by one, swaying under the pointer. Left two-thirds: the gate sign, name in huge brush lettering on two lines, role line painted beneath, one sentence, two brush buttons ("Email Sworup" saffron, "Walk the stalls" blue outline). Right third: a hanging marker-card cluster of three dated proof cards swinging on strings. Desktop left rail is the market map with the lit current district.

FORM: Night-Market Sign River (bolder-register deal, lead challenger; not from my grounded list). Seed key 628b92d7.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: the bulb string is a verlet rope that reacts to the pointer; hovering a stall routes a chase of light down the river to it. Motion grammar: signs swing from their top pivot, steam drifts, bulbs flicker on; reduced motion shows everything lit and still.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
